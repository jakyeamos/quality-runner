Use `tests/test_fleet_dynamic.py` for dynamic state handling,
`tests/test_maturity_feed.py` for outcome and feed compatibility,
`tests/test_change_matrix_maturity.py` for cross-surface contracts, and
`tests/test_strict_debt.py` for strict-policy evidence. Each behavior change
needs a positive case and its blocked, absent, or ambiguous boundary.
